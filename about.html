About Me

Hi, I'm Frankly

I've always been the kind of person who enjoys figuring out how things work—and then finding a better way to explain them.

My career began in customer technical support, where I developed a passion for solving complex problems and helping others do the same. Over time, I became the teammate people turned to for troubleshooting guidance, process clarification, and coaching. That passion eventually led me into Learning & Development, where I now create documentation, learning resources, workflow guides, and interactive training experiences that help technical support professionals succeed.

What motivates me most is making complex information approachable. Whether I'm building an interactive simulator in Figma, mapping a process in Canva, or developing decision-support tools in Microsoft Forms, my goal is the same: create practical resources that build confidence and make work easier.

I'm continually expanding my skills in instructional design, user experience, AI-assisted content creation, and process improvement, and I'm excited to keep growing in roles where thoughtful design can make a meaningful difference.
